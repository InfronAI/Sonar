# Explain a margin change to the board

Draft a board note reconciling the margin change and proposing the next piece of analysis.

## Case packet

Prior-quarter sales were $1.00m and gross profit $300k. Current sales are $1.10m and gross profit $297k. Finance attributes $30k additional profit to sales at the previous margin, a $22k mix loss and an $11k freight loss. Freight includes a $6k one-off disruption. Product-level volumes and supplier invoices have not yet been reconciled.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet. A rubric judge evaluates the content of the submitted file.
