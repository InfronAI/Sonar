# Bridge a six-week cash gap

Write a decision memo to the treasurer. Select a bridge action, justify the six-week cash floor and give a trigger for revisiting the plan.

## Case packet

The opening balance is $110k. Receipts and outflows through week six are $40k and $105k. Policy requires at least $60k at week six. A $20k supplier deferral costs $1k now. A $30k credit draw costs $2k now and matures in week eight. A $25k expected customer payment arrives in week seven and may slip.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet.
