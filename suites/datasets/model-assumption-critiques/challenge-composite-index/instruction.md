# Challenge a composite index

Advise a committee choosing between two sites under a two-criterion index.

## Case packet

On a 0–100 scale, site A scores 80 for access and 50 for cost performance. Site B scores 60 and 80 respectively. The index weights access by w and cost by 1−w. A committee favors w=0.7 but another group favors w=0.4. Scores carry several points of measurement uncertainty.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet. A rubric judge evaluates the content of the submitted file.
