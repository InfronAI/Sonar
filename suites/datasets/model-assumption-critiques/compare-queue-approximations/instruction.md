# Assess a queue approximation

Write a short modeling review before an information desk changes staffing.

## Case packet

An analyst fits a stationary single-server queue with 8 arrivals/hour and capacity 10/hour. Under the supplied model the mean time in system is 1/(capacity minus arrivals) hours. Counts over three successive hours were 3, 6 and 15. Staff take a 15-minute break during the busiest hour. The desk has not recorded individual waiting times.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet. A rubric judge evaluates the content of the submitted file.
