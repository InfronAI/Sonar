# Review a solar supply offer

Draft a purchasing note assessing a simplified solar supply proposal.

## Case packet

The offer supplies an estimated 100 MWh yearly at $80/MWh with payment on all production. The site expects to use 65 MWh directly, replacing imports at $130/MWh. Surplus earns $35/MWh. A sensitivity case reduces direct use to 45 MWh. Maintenance is included. Contract data on curtailment and price escalation is missing.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet. A rubric judge evaluates the content of the submitted file.
