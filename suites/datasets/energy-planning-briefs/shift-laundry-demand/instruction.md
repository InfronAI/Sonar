# Shift laundry demand outside the peak

Prepare a facilities decision on a proposed load shift for a residential building.

## Case packet

A laundry bank draws 24 kW for two hours. Peak electricity costs $0.30/kWh and off-peak $0.12/kWh. Running after 22:00 conflicts with the quiet-hours policy. A daytime off-peak window exists on Saturday. Daily use varies, and moving demand does not change the energy per load. A peak-demand fee is based on the highest monthly half hour.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet. A rubric judge evaluates the content of the submitted file.
