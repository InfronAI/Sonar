# Compare reading support pilots

Advise a school committee how to decide the next phase of two reading pilots.

## Case packet

School A gave tutoring to 20 volunteers and its mean reading score increased by 8 points. School B used classroom practice with 80 students and gained 5 points. A began 9 points lower. Different teachers marked the two follow-up tests. The district can fund a next-term pilot at four schools.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet. A rubric judge evaluates the content of the submitted file.
