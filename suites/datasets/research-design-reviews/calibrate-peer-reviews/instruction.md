# Calibrate a peer-review process

Design a short calibration exercise for reviewers selecting student projects.

## Case packet

Four reviewers each rated ten different projects on a five-point scale. Reviewers' mean ratings range from 2.8 to 4.1. Each reviewer saw one subject area only. A second round can afford 24 ratings on eight anonymized projects. The committee wants to distinguish severity differences from project quality and keep a usable appeals record.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet. A rubric judge evaluates the content of the submitted file.
