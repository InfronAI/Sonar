# Set a reserve draw rule

Prepare a reserve policy note for a small nonprofit board.

## Case packet

Unrestricted cash is $180k and monthly unavoidable spending is $50k. Board policy targets three months of unrestricted cash. A grant payment of $80k is restricted to a future program. A one-off repair of $45k is due this month, with an optional two-month payment plan costing $3k extra. No income is committed next month.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet.
