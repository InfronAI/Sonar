# Protect appointment continuity during a move

Write a scheduling handoff for a fictional clinic moving between buildings. Address access and continuity only.

## Case packet

On move day site A can host 18 visits and site B 16. There are 40 confirmed bookings including eight patients who requested step-free entrance. Only site B has a verified step-free route. Ten patients have consented to a remote appointment, but the clinic can host just six remote visits. Four of the ten also requested step-free access. Staff do not know whether the other six remote volunteers can all connect.
