# Assess a rain gauge change

Write a methods note for a regional rainfall series after an instrument relocation.

## Case packet

A gauge moved from a sheltered site to an exposed site on 1 July. Its mean daily reading fell from 5.0 to 4.0 mm across the move. A nearby unchanged gauge fell from 5.5 to 5.0 mm. The comparison spans different parts of the rainy season. There were only three days with both old and new gauges running.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet.
