# Interpret a shoreline shift in imagery

Write a planning briefing on two shoreline images and the evidence needed before designating erosion.

## Case packet

The mapped waterline shifted landward 12 m. Image A was taken at low tide and B at high tide. The local intertidal slope is 1 vertical to 20 horizontal and the water level differed by 0.5 m. Each image has up to 2 m registration error. Construction decisions use a 5 m persistent retreat threshold.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet. A rubric judge evaluates the content of the submitted file.
