# Allocate limited courier capacity

Write a dispatch recommendation for a regional library courier service.

## Case packet

Two vans each carry 20 crates. North needs 22 crates including six reserved for a Tuesday event. South needs 24 including ten due Tuesday. East offers a paid partner trip for up to eight overflow crates on Monday. Regular vans deliver Monday, and no later movement is guaranteed before Tuesday. Fragile crates, four in South, cannot use the partner.
