# Design a packaging field trial

Prepare a pilot decision for a distribution manager balancing damage and packing time.

## Case packet

A new insert was used on 200 nearby shipments and four were damaged. The old insert served 300 long-distance shipments and 12 were damaged. The new insert adds 20 seconds packing time. The warehouse processes 1,800 shipments daily. Long routes handle more transfers. Each damage claim ties up a support worker.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet. A rubric judge evaluates the content of the submitted file.
