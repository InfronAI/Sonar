# Interpret an incomplete snow survey

Write a survey memo deciding what can be reported about basin snow cover this week.

## Case packet

The low zone covers 60% of area and has mean snow depth 10 cm from 12 plots. The high zone covers 40% and has mean 40 cm from three accessible plots. Two steep high-zone routes were unavailable. Last year's comparable area-weighted depth was 25 cm. No snow-density measurements were made.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet. A rubric judge evaluates the content of the submitted file.
