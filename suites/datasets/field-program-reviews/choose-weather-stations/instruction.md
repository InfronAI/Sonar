# Select sites for a temperature network

Prepare a field director's recommendation for adding two stations to a regional network.

## Case packet

Existing stations are three downtown rooftops. Candidate V is a rural valley with mains power. Candidate H is an exposed highland with reliable solar power. Candidate R is another downtown roof with excellent connectivity. Comparison to a 20-year downtown series matters, but the new objective is regional coverage during heat events. Budget supports two new sites and a two-week portable overlap.

## Submission

Write your response in English to `/app/submission.md` as plain UTF-8 Markdown (up to 16 KiB). Address the decision-maker, support your reasoning with the supplied facts and identify material uncertainty. Use only this fictional case packet. A rubric judge evaluates the content of the submitted file.
